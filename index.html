Custom Costuming

Victor Bellmore · Senior Project with Ms. Cook

Can I design, build, and deliver a custom costume as a commissioned project? Over six months I’m making a wearable mascot-style character costume for a client, with a head, hand pieces, working electronic displays, and custom 3-D printed plastics.

Introduction

I love big craft projects, and I’ve wanted to learn how to make costumes for a while. This project lets me learn patterning, designing, cutting, and sewing faux fur and fabric, then combine that with electronics and 3-D printing.

Essential questions

  • What are the basic skills for costume design?
  • How can I combine sewing with electronics and 3-D printed components?

Project goals

My finished product is a complete set of head and hand pieces for a client’s character design, with working electronic displays and custom-made hard plastics. It will show how I learned sewing, patterning, fabrication, and electronics.

Timeline

  • About three months to design, pattern the fabric, and make the custom plastics
  • About two months to assemble everything, including the electronics
  • One extra month in case of setbacks

Why this matters to me

Where I’m starting

My sewing experience is limited, mostly sewing patches onto clothing and mending. I know a decent amount about wiring and programming electronic displays.

Risks

I’ve never sewn anything bigger than four inches, and I’ve never successfully patterned a 3-D object, so patterning may be my biggest challenge.

Mentor Information

Kilcodo

Independent costume maker and artist · Kilcodo Costumes

Kilcodo has made custom costumes since 2008 and has more than 17 years of experience building one-of-a-kind, handmade costumes and mascots. Her work includes the kind of fur, patterning, and character fabrication my project requires.

Visit Kilcodo Costumes

How we worked together

We meet in person once a month, and we talk constantly over Telegram in between, so I can ask questions as soon as I get stuck.

Kilcodo has taught me about sewing, working with fur, and the patterning and construction techniques behind creating costumes.
